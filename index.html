Welcome!

I study puzzles in nonbank financial intermediation and their implications for the real economy and household behavior. Utilizing structural models and granular institutional or household data, I examine how capital flows and risk management strategies across intermediaries—including mutual funds, nonbank lenders, and insurers—shape financial fragility and wealth allocation.

Research Interests: Nonbanks, Macro Finance, Household Finance.
