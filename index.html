About Me

I study puzzles in nonbank financial intermediation and their implications for the real economy and household behavior. Utilizing structural models and granular institutional or household data, I examine how capital flows and risk management strategies across intermediaries—including mutual funds, nonbank lenders, and insurers—shape financial fragility and wealth allocation. My research pipeline spans from explaining the derivative usage of mutual funds to household insurance demand to evaluate their broader impacts on market stability and aggregate economic activity.

Research Interests: Nonbank Financial Intermediation, Macro Finance, Household Finance.
